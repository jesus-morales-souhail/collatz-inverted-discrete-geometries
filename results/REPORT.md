# Two maps on Euclidean and spherical embeddings

Abstract integer runs of \(f\) and \(g\), with path statistics in a log phase plane and on the unit sphere obtained from sliding windows of \(\log(1+|x|)\).

Typical findings:

- Almost all seeds in a large initial range reach \(\{1,2,4\}\) under \(f\) (consistent with extensive existing verifications; not a global proof).  
- Under \(g\), the fraction of seeds with \(x_{10}>x_0\) is near one half; many orbits still enter finite cycles at longer horizons.  
- Spherical path lengths tend to be larger under \(g\) than under \(f\) on the same samples.

See `two_maps_euclidean_spherical.json` and `figures/` for related plots generated from the original experiment tree.
